# Outcomet Product Loop Skill

Outcomet connects customer feedback to shipped product capabilities in a closed loop:

1. **Feedback & Evidence** (`feedback_search`, `evidence_search`): Ingest customer calls, tickets, and interviews; extract verbatim evidence chunks.
2. **Problems** (`problem_search`, `problem_create`, `problem_link_evidence`): Cluster evidence chunks into validated customer problems.
3. **Initiatives & Work Items** (`initiative_search`, `work_item_search`): Scope initiatives and link engineering work items.
4. **Capabilities & Changes** (`capability_search`, `change_search`): Map shipped PRs and changes back to the Living Product Tree.
